# M32 — Polish (0.19.1) — design

**Date:** 2026-10-06 · **Status:** approved in brainstorm, awaiting written-spec review
**Decisions:** none new (ADR-052 amended: sticky main GPU, HWiNFO mutex)

## Goal

The user chose a polish release with every deferred minor of M31 and the Free-layout bug found while setting up their
desktop (a Free fence written without cells shows its items arranged but saves their spots only at the next item
change). Ten items, one patch release. Hard rules stand; no new dependency; no config schema change.

## Items

1. **Accent bars follow the accent.** Every `WM_SETTINGCHANGE "ImmersiveColorSet"` rebuilds the widgets' bar brush in
   every fence (today only a light/dark flip rebuilds the theme). If WPF's `SystemColors.AccentColor*` still hold the old
   colour at that moment, the brush is built again at the next dispatcher pass.
2. **One buffer per reading.** The shared-memory copy is rented from `ArrayPool<byte>.Shared` and returned after parsing
   (the parsers take a span already); no ~90 KB array per 2-second reading.
3. **Sticky main GPU.** The chosen graphics card (by sensor index, or by adapter in the Windows fallback) stays chosen
   until another one uses clearly more video memory: at least twice as much and at least 256 MB more. Pure Core
   function, test-first (`Widgets.StickyGpu(previous, candidates)` or equivalent): no flip on a hybrid laptop whose
   graphics card idles at 0 MB while the integrated GPU holds a few hundred.
4. **No empty hint line.** A "—" tile shows its hint line only when it has a hint (CPU TEMP); GPU TEMP and RAM do not
   grow.
5. **HWiNFO under its lock.** The copy of `Global\HWiNFO_SENS_SM2` is taken while holding `Global\HWiNFO_SM2_MUTEX`
   (waited for at most 20 ms); a missing or busy mutex reads without it, as now.
6. **Clock date line in the culture's order.** When the culture's long date puts the weekday last (ja, zh, ko: its
   `LongDatePattern` ends with `dddd`), the line is "<month-day> <weekday>" ("10月5日 月曜日"); otherwise
   "<weekday>, <month-day>" as now ("Monday, 5 October", "Monday, October 5"). Test-first with ja-JP, zh-CN, ko-KR,
   en-GB, en-US, de-DE.
7. **The first-start notice never shows in a game**, also when a retried tray icon shows it later: game mode is checked
   when the notice would show.
8. **Free spots saved at first layout.** When a fence window with a Free layout has laid out (its columns are known),
   any of its items without a stored cell are pinned at the cells they show at and saved — not only at the next item
   change. Hidden tabs keep today's rule (pinned when their box shows them).
9. **Tidy-ups.** `WidgetOptions.Fahrenheit` written only when true (clocks no longer carry `"fahrenheit": false`); the
   missing space in `Widgets.Page`; `PercentWidth.Of80` removed and its summary corrected.
10. **Checklist.** Section AR: the accent change, the Free fence after a fresh config, the CJK date line, the hint lines;
    plus the M31 gaps: the two-GPU fallback at idle, Afterburner without its CPU temperature graph, HWiNFO sharing off
    (DEAD) and back.

## Testing

- Core (xUnit, test-first): the sticky choice (keeps the previous one; switches at 2× and +256 MB; first choice by most
  memory); the date line per culture; `Fahrenheit` omitted when false and round-tripped when true; the Free pin rule if it
  lands in Core.
- Live check (asked first): a Free fence written without cells is pinned and saved after start (the config shows cells);
  the date line in a CJK culture by test only (the user's PC is English); the accent change only if the user agrees to
  the script switching the Windows accent and back — otherwise by hand.

## Out of scope

New features (sensor picker, clock options); changes to the welcome or the guide beyond what an item needs.
